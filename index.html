How it works

Creators manage streams and recordings, review generated highlights, and prepare clips. Where TikTok integration is enabled and authorized, the application may transfer user-selected content to TikTok using its official developer interfaces.

Availability and behavior of individual features depend on the installed application version and TikTok permissions.
